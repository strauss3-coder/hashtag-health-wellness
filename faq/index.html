C1FAQ

Questions, answered.

Plain answers about membership, the lounge, our products and your privacy.

01Who can become a member?

Adults aged 18 and older who verify their identity and agree to the house code and membership terms. Every application is reviewed by our team.

02How long does joining take?

The application takes about 10 minutes. Verification is usually done in minutes, and we review applications within one business day.

03Why do you verify my identity?

We are an adults-only private club. Verification confirms that every member is who they say they are and is 18 or older. It protects you, other members and the club.

04Can I verify in person instead?

Yes. Bring your South African ID or passport to the lounge during opening hours and the team will complete it with you.

05Can two people share a membership?

Yes. The Duo membership covers two people for a year. Each person is verified individually.

06Can I bring a guest?

Guests must be members too, so they will need to join and be verified before visiting. The Monthly Pass is a good option for visitors.

07What is there to do in the lounge?

Pool, air hockey, an arcade machine, a gaming zone and a trolley of board games, with something on every night: trivia, board games, a Friday pool tournament and talent show, and Saturday DJ sessions. Pool and air hockey are paid per game: buy pool tokens at the counter.

08Are you a medical cannabis collection point?

Yes. We are an approved collection point in the Synergy Wellness Network for patients who already hold a Section 21 authorisation. We do not prescribe, and authorised products are handed over only to verified patients. To apply for the Hashtag Medical Patient Card, use the Synergy Wellness patient portal (linked on our Membership page). The card is renewed every 6 months and includes a full year of lounge access.

09Is alcohol allowed?

No. The lounge is alcohol-free, and firearms are not permitted on the premises.

10Are your CBD products legal?

Our open-shelf CBD products are chosen to fit South Africa’s rules for over-the-counter CBD, and every batch has a Certificate of Analysis. See our journal article on how CBD is regulated.

11Can CBD help with a medical condition?

We do not make medical claims, and our team cannot give medical advice. Please speak to your doctor or pharmacist, especially if you take medication.

12Can I order online?

Not yet. You can browse the catalogue online and buy in the lounge. Members will be able to reserve items for collection in the members’ area.

13What happens to my ID information?

We keep only what we need, protect it under POPIA, and limit who can see it. Our Privacy notice explains exactly what we keep, why and for how long.
