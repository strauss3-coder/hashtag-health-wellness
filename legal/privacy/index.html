Privacy policy (POPIA)

In short

  • We collect what you give us (name, contact details, date of birth, membership, bookings and payments) and basic website usage data.
  • We use it to run your membership, bookings and payments, to check age and identity, and to keep the club and its members safe.
  • We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers we need (such as payments and hosting) or when the law requires.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object, or opt out of marketing at any time.

Full text

Hashtag Health & Wellness respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you access or use our website, communicate with us, submit forms, request information, make bookings, apply for membership, or otherwise interact with us.

3.1 Interpretation

For purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  1. 3.1.1“Business” means Hashtag Health & Wellness (full name and citation).
  2. 3.1.2“Website” means the Hashtag Health & Wellness website and all related digital platforms operated by or on behalf of the Business.
  3. 3.1.3“Personal Information” means information relating to an identifiable, living natural person and, where applicable, an identifiable existing juristic person, as contemplated in applicable South African law.
  4. 3.1.4“User” means any person who accesses or uses the Website.
  5. 3.1.5“Member” means any person accepted into the private membership structure of the Business.

3.2 Information We May Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

  1. 3.2.1full name and surname
  2. 3.2.2phone number
  3. 3.2.3WhatsApp number
  4. 3.2.4email address
  5. 3.2.5physical address or area
  6. 3.2.6date of birth or age confirmation
  7. 3.2.7membership enquiry information
  8. 3.2.8booking and event information
  9. 3.2.9payment-related information
  10. 3.2.10communication records
  11. 3.2.11customer support records
  12. 3.2.12device information
  13. 3.2.13browser type and usage data
  14. 3.2.14IP address
  15. 3.2.15website interaction data
  16. 3.2.16any other information voluntarily submitted by you

3.3 How We Collect Information

Personal information may be collected when:

  1. 3.3.1you visit or browse the Website
  2. 3.3.2you complete a contact form
  3. 3.3.3you submit a membership enquiry or application
  4. 3.3.4you contact us by email, WhatsApp, phone, or social media
  5. 3.3.5you request a booking, reservation, or event access
  6. 3.3.6you make a payment or submit proof of payment
  7. 3.3.7you subscribe to updates or marketing
  8. 3.3.8you otherwise provide information directly to us

Some information may also be collected automatically through cookies, analytics tools, and standard website technologies.

3.4 Purposes for Which Information Is Used

We may collect, use, process, and store personal information for lawful business purposes, including to:

  1. 3.4.1respond to enquiries and communicate with you
  2. 3.4.2process membership-related requests
  3. 3.4.3assess membership applications
  4. 3.4.4manage bookings, reservations, and events
  5. 3.4.5verify identity, age, or eligibility where necessary
  6. 3.4.6process payments and maintain transaction records
  7. 3.4.7improve our website, services, and operations
  8. 3.4.8maintain internal administration and recordkeeping
  9. 3.4.9send important service-related notices
  10. 3.4.10send promotional or marketing communications where lawful
  11. 3.4.11protect our business, staff, members, and users
  12. 3.4.12detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  13. 3.4.13comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3.5 Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information where justified by one or more lawful grounds, including:

  1. 3.5.1your consent
  2. 3.5.2the performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps
  3. 3.5.3compliance with a legal obligation
  4. 3.5.4protection of a legitimate business interest
  5. 3.5.5protection of your legitimate interests or those of the Business
  6. 3.5.6any other lawful basis recognised under applicable law

3.6 Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell and undertake not to sell personal information to third parties.

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary or legally obliged to do so to:

  1. 3.6.1payment processors and payment service providers
  2. 3.6.2website hosting providers and IT service providers
  3. 3.6.3booking or communication platforms used by the Business
  4. 3.6.4professional advisors
  5. 3.6.5law enforcement or competent authorities where legally required
  6. 3.6.6service providers assisting with business administration or support

3.7 Cookies and Website Analytics

The Website may use cookies, pixels, analytics, and similar technologies to:

  1. 3.7.1improve website functionality
  2. 3.7.2remember user preferences
  3. 3.7.3analyse website traffic and usage
  4. 3.7.4improve performance and content
  5. 3.7.5support security and fraud prevention

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but doing so may affect Website functionality.

3.8 Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no website, digital platform, email transmission, server, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3.9 Retention of Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for:

  1. 3.9.1the purpose for which it was collected
  2. 3.9.2business and operational recordkeeping
  3. 3.9.3legal, tax, audit, compliance, or dispute purposes
  4. 3.9.4enforcement of our rights and policies

3.10 Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  1. 3.10.1request access to personal information we hold about you
  2. 3.10.2request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  3. 3.10.3object to certain forms of processing
  4. 3.10.4request deletion where legally permissible
  5. 3.10.5withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  6. 3.10.6lodge a complaint with the relevant authority if you believe your rights have been infringed

3.11 Children and Age-Restricted Access

This Website is not intended for persons under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons who do not meet the applicable age requirement.

3.12 Third-Party Links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, payment systems, social media services, or communication tools.

We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices, policies, content, or security of such third parties.

3.13 Cross-Border Processing

Some of our website, hosting, analytics, communication, or software service providers may store or process information outside South Africa.

By using the Website and submitting personal information, you acknowledge and consent to such transfer, storage, or processing where lawful and reasonably necessary for our operations.

3.14 Direct Marketing

Where lawful, we may send Users promotional messages, club updates, event notices, or marketing communications.

Users may opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe process or contacting us directly.

3.15 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to amend, update, or replace this Privacy Policy at any time.

Any updated version will become effective upon publication on the Website, unless otherwise stated.
