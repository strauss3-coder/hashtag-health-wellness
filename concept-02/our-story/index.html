B8What we stand for

Four things we won’t compromise.

  1. 01

    Adults only, always

    Every member is verified. It keeps the lounge safe and private for everyone.

  2. 02

    Honest about the law

    We follow it, explain it in plain language, and update our guidance when it changes.

  3. 03

    Clear products

    Brands we trust, clear labels, and no promises a product can’t keep.

  4. 04

    A tribe, not a crowd

    Let’s vibe and grow our tribe: game nights, talent shows and Saturday sessions with the people who keep coming back.
