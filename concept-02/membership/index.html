Why we check, what we keep.

  • Identity checks are done by a trusted verification partner, or in person at the counter.
  • We keep the result of the check, not your biometric data.

Questions

Membership, answered.

01Who can become a member?

Adults aged 18 and older who verify their identity and agree to the house code and membership terms. Every application is reviewed by our team.

02How long does joining take?

The application takes about 10 minutes. Verification is usually done in minutes, and we review applications within one business day.

03Why do you verify my identity?

We are an adults-only private club. Verification confirms that every member is who they say they are and is 18 or older. It protects you, other members and the club.

04Can I verify in person instead?

Yes. Bring your South African ID or passport to the lounge during opening hours and the team will complete it with you.

05Can two people share a membership?

Yes. The Duo membership covers two people for a year. Each person is verified individually.

06Are you a medical cannabis collection point?

Yes. We are an approved collection point in the Synergy Wellness Network for patients who already hold a Section 21 authorisation. We do not prescribe, and authorised products are handed over only to verified patients. The Medical Patient Card membership exists for these patients.
