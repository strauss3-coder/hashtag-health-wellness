A1The Lounge

A room to slow down in.

Booths, warm light, a pool table and a quiet corridor. Open seven days a week for verified adult members.

Open7 days a week

A2The space

Set out like a good lounge bar, without the bar: booths along one wall, the games room at the back, the quiet corridor between. Capacity, refreshments and music details to be confirmed.

A3What’s inside

Everything a slow evening needs.

  • Booth seating

    Deep leather booths and low tables for unhurried evenings.

  • Pool & air hockey

    A full-size pool table and an air hockey table, free for members.

  • Arcade & gaming

    A retro arcade cabinet and a console gaming zone.

  • Board games

    Chess, Scrabble, 30 Seconds and more on the trolley.

  • The quiet corridor

    A calm, plant-lined space away from the games room.

  • Members only

    Every guest is a verified adult member. No exceptions.

A4The games room

Play, quietly competitive.

Pool practice and video games every day, a Friday tournament, and an arcade cabinet that has settled more than one argument.

B3The house code

Four rules keep it calm.

  • Adults 18+ only

    Strictly no under-18s, with verified membership at the door.

  • No alcohol

    The lounge is an alcohol-free space.

  • No firearms

    Firearms are not permitted on the premises.

  • Respect the space

    Keep it calm and courteous, and keep what happens here private.
