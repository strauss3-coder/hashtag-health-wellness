The lounge, and how we keep it calm

A short guide to the house code: why it exists and what it asks of every member.

17 September 2026 · 3 min read

The lounge works because everyone in it wants the same thing: somewhere quiet, private and comfortable to slow down. The house code is short, and it keeps it that way.

The house code

  • Adults 18+ only, with verified membership.
  • No alcohol on the premises.
  • No firearms on the premises.
  • Keep the volume low and the space tidy.
  • What happens in the lounge stays private. No photos of other members.

If you are unsure about anything, ask the team at the counter. We would rather you ask than guess.
