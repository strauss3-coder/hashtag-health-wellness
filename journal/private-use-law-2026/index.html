Private use, explained: where the law stands in 2026

From the 2018 Constitutional Court judgment to the 2026 draft regulations, what has changed and what has not.

24 September 2026 · 5 min read · Reviewed 1 October 2026

This article is awaiting legal review before launch.

Cannabis law in South Africa has moved a long way since 2018, but it is still a work in progress. Here is where things stand, in plain language.

2018: private use

In 2018 the Constitutional Court ruled that adults may use, possess and cultivate cannabis in private for their own use.

2024: the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act

In May 2024 the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act was signed into law. It sets out what counts as private use. Much of the detail depends on regulations that are still being finalised.

2026: draft regulations

The first draft regulations were published for public comment in February 2026. They are drafts: the final numbers may change.

What has not changed

  • Use in public is not permitted.
  • Selling cannabis outside licensed medical channels is not permitted.
  • Everything is adults-only.

We update this article whenever the law changes. It is general information, not legal advice.
