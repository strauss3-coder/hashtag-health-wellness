A2Liner notes

A wellness lounge with good taste and a clear conscience.

Hashtag began with a simple idea: adults deserve somewhere warm, private and well kept to slow down. The look is retro. The standards are not.

  • Verified adults only

    Every member’s identity and age is confirmed before they set foot in the lounge.

  • Clearly labelled products

    CBD content, THC limit and batch on every CBD product, with a lab report on request.

  • Approved Section 21 collection point

    Part of the Synergy Wellness Network for patients who hold a SAHPRA Section 21 authorisation.

  • Privacy, properly

    We keep only what we need, never ask about your health, and protect it under POPIA.
